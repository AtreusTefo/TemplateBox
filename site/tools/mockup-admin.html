Mockup Coordinate Picker

Internal tool. Loads a base image locally (nothing is uploaded), records the four print-area corners, and emits a ready-to-paste entry for js/mockup-templates.js.

Load a base image, then click the print area's corners in order: top-left, top-right, bottom-right, bottom-left. For a "window" base, the corners are the edges of the transparent opening (shown as checkerboard).
