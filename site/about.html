About TemplateBox

Operating since 2026

TemplateBox is a set of free document tools that run entirely inside your web browser. There is no account to create, no trial to start, no export limit, and no watermark on anything you produce. There is also no server holding your data, because there is no server at all.

What you can make here

  • Invoices with line items, automatic tax and a calculated balance due
  • Rent receipts, cash payment receipts, itemized business receipts and printable sales receipt forms
  • Employee warning notices with warning levels, violation categories and signature blocks
  • Resumes and CVs that export as machine-readable PDFs
  • Framed photo posters and print-on-demand product mockups

How the browser-only architecture works

Conventional document tools send what you type to a server, process it there, and send a file back. That architecture requires an account so the server knows whose data is whose, and it means your invoice, your tenant's address or your employee's disciplinary record sits in somebody else's database.

TemplateBox does the work on your machine instead. The editors are ordinary web pages; the document preview is rendered by JavaScript in your browser as you type; the PDF is compiled locally by a library running in the same page; and the finished file is written straight to your downloads folder. Nothing is transmitted because there is nowhere to transmit it to.

Your in-progress work is held in your browser's own local storage on your device, which is why you can close the tab and come back to an unfinished document. Clearing your browser's site data for this domain erases it permanently, and we cannot recover it, because we never had a copy.

Why it is free

The tools are funded by advertising shown on a short interstitial page between choosing a template and opening the editor. That interval is the entire business model. It is why there is no subscription, no export cap, and no watermark.

Advertising here is always a passive banner: it sits beside the page on a wide screen, or along the bottom edge on a phone, and nothing else. No pop-ups, nothing that opens a tab you did not ask for, nothing that moves, covers or delays what you are working on. A pop-under ad ran on this site until August 2026 and was removed because it redirected visitors to other sites on ordinary clicks; that category of advertising will not return.

An editor cluttered with advertising while someone drafts a disciplinary notice is a bad tool, so the editors carry one quiet banner and no more.

That banner is rendered inside an isolated frame served from the advertising network's own domain, which browsers prevent from reading the page around it. It cannot see your document, your form fields, or anything saved in your browser for this site. Ad formats that would run inside the editor page itself are deliberately not used.

Whatever you export is untouched: no watermark, no advertising, no branding you did not put there yourself.

What we can and cannot see

We cannot see any document you create. We do not receive names, addresses, amounts, employee records, resume contents, or uploaded images, and this is an architectural fact rather than a policy promise.

What we do receive is ordinary web traffic information through third-party services: hosting and DNS logs, anonymous interaction analytics used to find usability problems, and the advertising network's own measurement. The privacy policy sets out each of these in detail.

Accuracy and limitations

The guidance published on this site and in the guides section is general information. Requirements for receipts, invoices and employment records vary considerably between countries and between states or provinces, and they change. Nothing here is legal, tax or employment advice, and a consequential decision warrants professional advice specific to your jurisdiction.

The tools produce documents; they do not verify that a document meets a particular legal requirement where you are.
